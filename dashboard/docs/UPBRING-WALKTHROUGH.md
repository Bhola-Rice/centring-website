# Upbring grant workspace

Open Settings to see Upbring’s mission, programs in Texas and public sources. My profile holds the current reviewer’s details. The team uses assignment roles for grants, finance and program delivery.

In Documents, open the caregiver navigation planning brief. It distinguishes proposed activities from the budget, locations and dates that still need approval. Use Opportunities to review a notice against the organisation and its project, then open a draft and inspect a cited passage.

In Applications, the Family Navigation Pilot includes a sample submission and its supporting budget. The funding scenarios illustrate preparation, submission and award tracking. Their amounts and outcomes are examples for the walkthrough.
