# Upbring grant workspace

Open Settings to see Upbring’s mission, programs in Texas and public sources. My profile holds the current reviewer’s details. The team uses assignment roles for grants, finance and program delivery.

In Documents, open the caregiver navigation planning brief. It distinguishes proposed activities from the budget, locations and dates that still need approval. Use Opportunities to review a notice against the organisation and its project, then open a draft and inspect a cited passage.

In Applications, the Family Navigation Pilot includes a sample submission and its supporting budget. The funding scenarios illustrate preparation, submission and award tracking. Their amounts and outcomes are examples for the walkthrough.

## After the award

Open **Awards & reports → Open client demo**. The Family Navigation Pilot models a win against the existing six-month budget note. All award decisions, transactions, reporting dates and results in the walkthrough are illustrative. The demo is held in memory for the session.

1. Open **Demo portfolio**. The two examples have $42,000 awarded, $24,970 of recorded spend and $17,030 remaining as of November 20, 2026. Select Family Navigation Pilot.
2. Trace the $30,000 award to its planning budget. The ledger shows $13,720 spent, $16,280 remaining, and the person or team responsible for each charge. The dashed chart line assumes even spending across the award period.
3. Open the missing reference on the November travel reimbursement. Enter an example receipt reference and watch the attention message update. Use **Ask Beedy** to discuss the current figures when the workspace AI connection is available.
4. Open the interim report draft. Its period ends October 31, so its spending is $13,330. The $390 November charge belongs to a later period. Review the narrative and outcome evidence, then download the working paper. The grant team uses the agreement to determine which funder form to file.
5. To demonstrate the handoff from Applications, open the **Family Navigation Pilot** submitted example and record an award. Select **Open award record** to confirm the agreement, dates, budget lines and owner. This path saves changes in the current workspace. Use a demonstration workspace for rehearsal.

Finance staff can post individual charges and credits or import a finance CSV from **Record expense**. The import previews net dollars and new rows before posting, and checks transaction IDs against the award ledger. Each award has its own reporting periods. Filing records capture the submission reference and preserve the report figures at the time the user records the filing.

The [Grants.gov reporting overview](https://www.grants.gov/learn-grants/grant-reporting.html) and [post-award forms library](https://www.grants.gov/forms/forms-repository/post-award-reporting-forms) describe financial and performance reporting for federal awards. Use each actual funder's instructions for its dates, format, evidence and submission process.
